VITESS Module Filter

The module filter selects trajectories with certain parameter values. It is a numerical operation that does not have an equivalent in reality, (though some instrument components have a similar task). Only trajectories with a value of the chosen parameter within the given range are considered further. For sufficient flexibility, up to 4 parameter can be chosen and combined logically with AND, OR or the combination: par1 AND par2 OR par3 AND par4.
